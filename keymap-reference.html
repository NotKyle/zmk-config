Flashing order

To get a half into bootloader mode, double-tap its reset button — within about half a second. It mounts as a USB drive called NICENANO; drag the .uf2 on and it flashes and reboots by itself. Each half is its own device, so plug in and flash one at a time. Once this firmware is on, BOOT on the Numbers layer does the same thing without the button.

  1. settings_reset to both halvesNot optional. ZMK Studio is enabled with locking off, so any keymap you ever edited in Studio is saved to settings and silently overrides the .keymap file. Skip this and you may see no change at all.
  2. lily58_left, then lily58_rightDouble-tap reset again for each — settings_reset leaves the board running, not in bootloader. Artifacts are attached to the green Actions run on the fix/keymap-chatter-and-bt-disconnect branch.
  3. Re-pair BluetoothThe reset clears all five profiles. Pick a profile on the Numbers layer top row before pairing.

If something still feels wrong

A key still repeats or drops characters

Raise CONFIG_ZMK_KSCAN_DEBOUNCE_RELEASE_MS from 10 toward 12–15 in config/lily58.conf. Release debounce is the one that governs doubled letters. If it is only one or two specific keys, that is a worn switch or a cold solder joint and no config value will fix it.

A modifier thumb is a stretch

⌘ is on the left thumb and ⌃ on the right outer thumb, so neither competes with ⏎ or ⌫ for a home position. Give it three days — thumb maps feel wrong before they feel fast.

You miss having mods on the home row

Left-hand home row mods were deliberately left off: they are timing-based, and timing is what was broken. Right-hand mods can be added positionally later if the thumbs prove not to be enough.

Gone for good: the SPC+⏎ combo. At a 75 ms window it fired during normal typing and ate both keys, and it collided with Doom's SPC leader. Numbers is a conditional layer now — hold both thumbs, no timeout.
